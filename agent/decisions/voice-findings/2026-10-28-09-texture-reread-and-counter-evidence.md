## Important counter-evidence about the rejected hypotheses
The "slop tells" listed in finding 08 are NOT absent from his own essays:
- "Instead of asking X, I want to ask Y. Instead of optimizing for scale, I want to optimize for delight." (2025-06-04, ending section)
- "The question isn't whether we're getting dumber or smarter. The question is: what kind of intelligence are we building..." (2025-06-17, near the end)
- "AI is useful, but human dedication is meaningful." and "People care about people." (2025-07-29)
- Closing questions: "how about you challenge yourself to put your story out there?" (2025-06-11)
- Bold, slogan-like thesis lines: "The future belongs to the storytellers." (2025-06-11)
So v5 removes them because he rejected v4, but removing them is a bet, not a measured rule. What likely differs is placement: in his essays these lines come after concrete material and in his own rhythm. In v4 they were stacked with no scene. He also wrote about LLM tells himself (2025-07-29: "vibrant tapestries", bullet-pointed posts, excessive em-dashes), which is a better source for negative examples than my guesses.
