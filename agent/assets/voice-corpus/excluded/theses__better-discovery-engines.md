Engagement-based algorithms got us stuck in a loop. I believe we can build more efficient discovery if we lean on people's relationships to one another for our feeds.

> **Note:** The problem isn't recommendation — it's the optimization target. Engagement maximization is at odds with genuine discovery. Social graphs are a better signal than time-on-platform.
