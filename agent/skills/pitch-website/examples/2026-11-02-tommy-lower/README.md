## Provenance and limits

- Published as a temporary anonymous page (here.now, expired 2026-11-03).
  The owner-only claim link is deliberately not stored anywhere in this
  repo. The URL inside the accompanying message is dead after expiry; that
  is expected - the bundle here is the reference, not the link.
- The bundle is the working source (single self-contained file), not a
  live-render recovery.
- Public-fit case: the page names the recipient and references a firm's
  public description with the owner's explicit request that approved pages
  be saved as reference. Internal review reports, the private research
  brief and pre-approval drafts are excluded; they live only in the
  owner's private archive (Kairon Drive pitch-archive).
- Status: approved example of the pitch method applied to a personal
  methods introduction. Not a client result; no business outcome is
  established or implied. The Soteris walkthrough on the page is an
  illustrative sketch from public descriptions, not the firm's private
  workflow - the page says so itself.

## What to copy from this example

- The governing idea is the owner's own expression, framed as an aim, not
  a product guarantee.
- One clearly labeled illustrative example carried through the five depth
  rungs, machine/person roles always visible, slots on demand.
- Working rules quoted as his, with judgment-vs-script distinction intact.
- Restraint: no eyebrows, no decoration, plain personal opening.

## What not to copy

- The recipient-specific content and the personal greeting. Rebuild from
  fresh prospect research for each reader.
- The expiring URL. Every new pitch gets its own here.now page.
