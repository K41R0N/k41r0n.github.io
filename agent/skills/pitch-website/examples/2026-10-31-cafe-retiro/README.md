# Example: Cafe Retiro pitch page (2026-10-31)

The first end-to-end live run of `skills/pitch-website` on a real business:
Cafe Retiro, a specialty coffee shop in El Retiro, Antioquia, that the owner
visited the same morning. Research brief, first page, nine rounds of owner
corrections, accepted the same day. This folder is the regression evidence
for the pitch method: the next run should need fewer corrections, and this
case is how that claim gets checked.

## Files

- `index.html` - the accepted final page, verbatim as published (Spanish,
  interactive five-stage walkthrough with the seven attention parts visible
  at each stage), minus the temporary host's injected social meta tags.
  Expects `cafe-retiro-home.jpg` beside it.
- `cafe-retiro-home.jpg` - the hero photo the page references (the
  business's own public photography; kept so the example renders, not for
  reuse elsewhere).
- `prior-preinteractive.html` - the immediately earlier page source, before
  the interactive demonstration was added. Kept as evidence of what
  correction 7 (show, don't tell) changed.
- `research-brief.md` - the prospect research brief behind the page (the
  `prospect-research` method's output). Public copy: the observations
  derived from the owner's private visit photos are withheld here; the
  byte-for-byte original lives only in the private Drive backup.
- `corrections-record.md` - the verbatim, timestamped record of the nine
  owner corrections, the acceptance, and the storage correction, with the
  correction-to-gate and correction-to-method mappings.

## What to copy from this example

- Language: written entirely in the reader's language (Spanish), including
  the method itself. The five depth stages and the seven attention parts
  appear as plain questions and everyday words, never as English labels or
  technical terms.
- Structure: the reader's why first; one clearly illustrative example (a
  post-class follow-up) carried through the five stages; the seven parts of
  the work visible at every stage; the weakest-part rule; the
  two-manual-runs gate before any scripted repetition; one call to action.
- Honesty: the worked example is labeled as supposition, not as the
  business's real process. No invented ROI, no implied problems, sources
  linked to the business's own pages.
- Design: calm editorial layout in the business's own palette. No eyebrows,
  stickers, or decorative elements.

## What not to copy

- The business-specific content. Rebuild from fresh prospect research for
  each reader.
- The live URL shape. The original page was a temporary site; every new
  pitch gets its own.
