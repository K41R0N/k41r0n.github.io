# Café Retiro | prospect research brief

Research date: 31 October 2026. Scope: public sources. The original brief also drew on owner-provided visit photos; those photo-derived observations are private and are withheld from this public copy. The byte-for-byte original is kept only in the private Drive backup.

## Decision
Do not propose or send a service offer yet. Café Retiro has a broad, coherent farm-to-cup story and multiple visitor/customer pathways. A small discovery conversation could test whether one path deserves attention, but no stated near-term goal or evidence of a current problem was found. Any pitch is a concept for Alejandro to review, not an assertion that Café Retiro needs a fix.

Leadership/org chart is unverified. A third-party LinkedIn result has conflicting employee figures and is excluded.

## Improvement hypotheses
1. INFERRED (low confidence): Because the public offering spans in-store coffee, packaged products, online ordering, classes and institutional formats, a deliberately connected “taste/learn here, choose a coffee to take home” pathway might be useful if that is an owner-selected goal. Validate with owner-selected priority, observed customer journeys, anonymized analytics and customer questions. Disconfirm if the existing path already works well, the visitor experience is intentionally non-commercial, or this is not a priority.
2. INFERRED (low confidence): The class and tasting offer could support a follow-up recommendation to a bean or brewing product when the business wants that outcome. Validate with owner-selected objective, current attendance/follow-up practices and conversion/repeat data. Disconfirm if education and cultural exchange are the intended outcome without retail follow-through, or if the current process already performs well.
3. INFERRED (very low confidence): Shopify and a legacy-looking WordPress catalog route may create duplicate discovery or conflicting product information, but may simply be old pages. Check live links/redirects, search indexing, support questions and analytics before suggesting any consolidation. Disconfirm if routes resolve consistently and visitors are not confused.

These are conditional test questions, not discovered pain points or recommendations. First ask what outcome Café Retiro wants to improve, if any.
