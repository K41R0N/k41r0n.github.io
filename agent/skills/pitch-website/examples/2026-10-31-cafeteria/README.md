# Example: first live pitch page (2026-10-31)

The approved result of the first end-to-end live run of `skills/pitch-website` on a real business: a specialty coffee shop the owner visited the same morning. Built, corrected through nine rounds of owner feedback, and accepted the same day.

- `index.html` - the full page, anonymized for this public repo. The business's name, its own source links, and its photography are removed. The page expects a `photo.jpg` beside it that is intentionally not committed (it is the business's own photography).
- The verbatim page, the photo, and provenance notes are in the private Drive archive: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1iCwtl7_6DP9F194H_nqoPT0KtwjNO_71
- The generalized retrospective of the run: `agent/knowledge/site/pitch-page-learnings-first-live-run-2026-10-31.md` (the verbatim correction record stays private in the Drive archive)

## What to copy from this example

- Language: written entirely in the reader's language (Spanish), including the method itself. The five depth stages and the seven attention parts appear as plain questions and everyday words, never as English labels or technical terms.
- Structure: reader's why first; one clearly illustrative example (a post-class follow-up) carried through the five stages; the seven parts of the work visible at every stage; the weakest-part rule; the two-manual-runs gate before any scripted repetition; one call to action.
- Honesty: the example is labeled as supposition, not as the business's real process. No invented ROI, no implied problems, sources linked to the business's own pages (kept in the private archive).
- Design: calm editorial layout in the business's own palette. No eyebrows, stickers, or decorative elements.

## What not to copy

- The business-specific content. Rebuild from fresh prospect research for each reader.
- The live URL shape. The original page was a temporary here.now site (expired or claimed); every new pitch gets its own.

Status: illustrative example, owner-reviewed. Not a deployed system, not a client result, no performance claims.
