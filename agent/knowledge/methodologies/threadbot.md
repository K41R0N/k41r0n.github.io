# The threadbot methodology

Documented 2026-10-29 from his own words (interview answer, WhatsApp), for the
methodology explainer service: when someone asks how he keeps posting, this is
the piece to hand them. Plain language, no framework names.

## Why he uses it

Finding time to post is the hardest part of his job. Deep work is the problem:
flow state and over-research eat the hours, and the feed goes quiet. And
social works best when there is something to bounce off of - a prompt beats a
blank page.

## Why he built it

A small bot that asks him questions grounded in his real work - what he
actually built that day. His replies become the content. The material is
already true before anyone writes a word, so there is no slop machine to
feed: the bot asks, he answers, the answer is the post.

## How to adopt it

For anyone struggling to come up with content:

1. Set up something that asks you about what you are actually building and
   why - a bot, a friend, a scheduled question in your calendar.
2. Answer it in your own words, the way you would explain it to one person.
3. Your answers are your posts. Trim, do not rewrite.

The point: you never need content ideas. You need someone asking about the
work you already did today.
