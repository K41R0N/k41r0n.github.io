## Executive summary

ESTABLISHED: ESD is a riverside ecopark on the Santo Domingo river canyon at Km 6 of the San Francisco road, municipality of Cocorná, Oriente Antioqueño, about two hours from Medellín. It sells day visits ("día de sol"), camping, lodging, and restaurant/bar service, with direct river access. A RUES mirror lists the registered company ECO SANTO DOMINGO SAS (NIT 901498391) as active (mirror report, not yet confirmed on the official registry). Its strongest public asset is a verified Instagram account (@ecoparqueriosantodomingo, 15K followers). Its own website was unreachable in our access checks today, and Google Maps carries no website link.

PROVISIONAL: the business runs reservation-led operations through phone/WhatsApp and social channels; public evidence of a working digital booking channel was not confirmed in this run. The owned website is down and Maps carries no site link, so owned-channel presence is weak relative to third-party surfaces.

MAIN OPEN QUESTION: no current business goal is publicly stated, so no goal-linked recommendation is made here. Planning work should start from the owner's stated goal.
