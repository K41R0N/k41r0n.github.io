# Reader-specific pitch page: review learnings, 2026-11-02 (Tommy page)

## Purpose and status

Dated record of the second live pitch-page run: a personal page introducing
the advice harness to a friend. Records the owner's corrections and their
root causes. Not a universal positioning statement; the accepted page and
its files are the reference (`skills/pitch-website/examples/2026-11-02-tommy-lower/`).

## Corrections and root causes

1. **here.now missed again.** The first preview went to Instinct Files; the
   here.now link then arrived late enough that he had to ask twice (1:18 AM,
   8:17 AM). Root cause: the 2026-10-31 "here.now by default" rule was
   routed as a preference, not enforced as the only surface, and no step
   checked it before the first link went out. Fix: the rule is now absolute
   in the skill (step 0, gate 0) - always here.now, never Instinct Files -
   and the approved page's files are stored as the reference.
2. **Copy method replaced with fact lint.** The first drafts claimed the
   five-pass method while running only deterministic checks; he caught it
   ("riddled with LLM writing patterns", "This is still AI copy"). Root
   cause: the harness's review routine was a 2026-10-30 reconstruction
   ("seven ordered passes") that matched no source of his and had dropped
   the creative passes, and the copy-five-pass pack's own source note said
   his pass files had never been read. Fix: his actual review framework was
   read at the source (reference doc + implementation), generalized into
   packs/copywriting-methods, the review routine rewritten to the real five
   passes, and the lint gate re-labeled as a gate. Review judges creative
   quality, voice source fit, and reader/audience/offer fit; a pass count
   certifies nothing.
3. **Method locations treated as known.** The marketing and ICP
   methodologies were not re-read at their source before positioning the
   page. Fix: knowledge/methodologies/method-sources.md now names where
   each process lives and its access rule; the skill's orientation step
   cites it.
4. **Personal note cut at approval.** The opening paragraph recounting the
   reader's own activity ("You've been bringing people together...") read
   as telling him what he already knows. One edit, then approved. Pattern:
   give the reader a reason the page is for him without narrating his own
   work back to him.

## What the accepted result did well

- Governing idea in the owner's own words ("an Alejandro on demand"),
  framed as an aim, not a product guarantee.
- The interactive ladder taught the delegation method on one task, roles
  always visible, detail behind a disclosure.
- Working rules quoted as his; illustrative example labeled as a sketch
  from public descriptions, with the professional sign-off boundary
  explicit.
