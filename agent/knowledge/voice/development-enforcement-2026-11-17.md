# Developed reasoning is required work

Implementation update, November 17, 2026. This changes the existing writing entrypoint, not the author's original methods. It follows the owner's request to update the writing process for consistent separate stages after a private essay improved through substantive development. No private essay, feedback export, predictions, client material or author-edited gold is included here.

## Required editorial work

The governing inquiry must come from the source and brief. Big-idea drafting, structural development, creative review and final judgment now require a development record:

- Three to five distinct reframes, each with a consequence, plus a selected frame and reason for choosing it.
- An exact source-substance anchor and an explanation of what is sourced versus newly reasoned. Voice examples cannot supply this anchor.
- Exact current-artifact spans and reasoning for an implication, the strongest objection, an example's warrant and limits, and gained ground.
- A scope boundary: what the argument has not established and what the author has not said.

An early drafting artifact may contain proposed reasoning; later artifacts must show how the thought developed. These fields cannot be replaced by a list of lens names or a statement that a pass happened. Source roles, exact quotations, distinct stage identities, immutable artifact references and changed-copy invalidation remain mandatory. The full brand-agnostic original creative methodology is retained alongside the earlier generalized reference. No client presets were imported.

These checks enforce the presence and linkage of required work. They do not prove that an objection is strong, an inference valid, a source fully understood or a draft good. Those questions remain critical editorial judgment and author evaluation. Synthetic tests deliberately use artificial responses and establish no creative success.

## Scope-aware application

Reflective essays need a developed inquiry and earned conclusions, not a forced sale, competitor, ideology or CTA. Landing-page ratios and headline budgets do not govern every format. A substantive request to flesh out an idea returns to drafting; it is not restricted to a polishing review's no-expansion rule. Neither route permits new autobiography, unsupported history or an invented author belief.

The positive answer deserves its full force before it is tested. A concrete example may establish possibility without predicting population outcomes. Gained ground must be stated at the strength the reasoning earns. Open questions are allowed; repeated suspension cannot substitute for development.

## Execution status and next-test procedure

`python3 agent/scripts/local/writing.py readiness` reports the current production blocker. It does not activate execution or authorize accounts, spending, installation or new author writing. The production configuration still has no executors and keeps new author writing disabled. An existing candidate adapter is review-only and its actual host conformance remains unverified.

The existing `init`, `prepare`, `execute`, `record`, `revise` and `packet` paths remain one sequence, not parallel methods. The next stage consumes the exact previous artifact. A new version invalidates downstream review. Review observes without rewriting. Distinct stage invocations are required; combined or retrospective declarations cannot claim dispatched completion.

A supervised private test can still follow the editorial stages honestly without buying a service. Prepare the source/brief, save each stage's actual work before beginning the next, retain immutable versions, conduct fitted whole-piece reviews and judge the revised whole. Record that work as manual, not dispatched. Imported manual evidence remains attested and cannot export a software-complete handoff. An incoming test must not be used as a software fixture or tuned against in advance.
