# Anti-pattern method: apply in context, preserve the speaker

Source-derived process adapter, 2026-11-16. The original supplied methods archive is preserved privately, unchanged. Its anti-slop standard combines a long-form editorial/tell registry, a deterministic gate, Peter Yang's no-ai-slop, and Every's ce-noslop. Original client mechanics and craft were read privately for reconciliation, not imported as personal voice rules. This adapter contains no client examples, claims, vocabulary or legal rules.

## Before writing

Choose author, edit or detect mode. In author mode, use the construction tests while drafting. In edit mode, preserve strong sentences and change only what fails. In detect mode, quote the pattern and explain a possible fix without rewriting or guessing whether AI wrote it.

## Review the architecture, not only words

Apply seven tests to a doubtful passage:

1. Mechanism: does it say what happens, rather than announce importance?
2. Portability: could the passage move unchanged to another speaker or subject?
3. Actor: is it clear who does the work, when the actor matters?
4. One idea: can the reader follow the sentence without holding unrelated claims?
5. Density: do different patterns accumulate or does one move repeat?
6. Decision first: does the opening give this reader the needed outcome?
7. Reader: does the reader have the context needed to understand or act?

Name paragraph moves before swapping words. Look for uniform paragraph topology, repeated restatements, predictable openers and endings, false contrast, significance narration, vague attribution, synonym cycling and tidy summaries that add nothing. Put a fact next to its consequence instead of asserting its importance. A second mention should add proof, a mechanism or a consequence. Preserve uncertainty that is real.

## Preservation and scope

Every fact, number, name, quotation, citation, identifier and source caveat survives. Source examples teach form, never facts or personal stories. Preserve distinctive vocabulary, humor, qualifiers, fragments and digressions when the selected speaker actually uses them. A list of candidate tells is not a personal ban list. Literal technical uses and quotations need context review, not automatic deletion.

The source standard's numerical budgets and platform rules belong to its format and speaker. They do not override Alejandro's actual samples or become universal personal sentence caps. Different source standards disagree on dash budgets, sentence ranges and density thresholds. Record the applicable scope and the conflict; do not merge the strictest values into a new invented rule. An isolated tell is not proof of bad writing or AI authorship.

## Evidence to retain

For each finding: exact draft span, source clause, pattern, why it fails for this reader and register, proposed fix, preservation check and review outcome. Re-review the paragraph after a structural revision and check the whole piece for regrowth. Unresolved factual or meaning changes return to source research. Taste and voice remain author judgments.
