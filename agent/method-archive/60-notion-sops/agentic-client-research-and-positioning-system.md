# Page content (verbatim Notion-flavored Markdown)

This is a multi-agent flow that grabs the minimum possible context about a new client (website, social accounts, POC, Contract, SOW, etc), and expands it into:

### Phase 0: Dossier Agent (Perplexity) → company\_dossier.json

Basic info gets turned into a breakdown of who they are, info about the company, competition, market, etc. It essentially enriches what we know about a client and turns it into a dossier.

### Phase 1-2: Master Agent (Claude) → Context + Questions

Turns the dossier output into<br /><br /> 1. A validated breakdown and expansion of the data that can be further researched based on their challenges (from client calls, etc).<br /><br />2. A list of \~50 Research questions following the DATA, LISTEN, TEAM structure to further clarify how to tackle their positioning (the idea here is to understand what their competition is doing right, how their audience behaves, what the company can claim, and how far can we stretch)

### Phase 3: Research Agent (Perplexity/Gemini) → Findings

Grabs the research questions from the previous phase, and gets data-backed answers for all of them sequentially.

### Phase 4: Master Agent (Claude) → Strategy Document

Synthesizes the Dossier, the research, the data, etc. into one document covering the gaps in the market, the potential ICP logic, and proposed Positioning angles based on the data (this is where actual creativity pops in, using this output and giving it a human twist)
