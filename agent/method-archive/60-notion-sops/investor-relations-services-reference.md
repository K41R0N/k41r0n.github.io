# IR Services Overview

## Core IR Service Categories

| Service Category | Key Deliverables | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <b>Investor-Facing Materials</b> | Investor decks, pitch decks, annual reports | Overview decks that articulate strategy and financial models; Quarterly earnings presentations; Investor day materials |
| <b>Strategic Communication</b> | Messaging strategy, positioning | Value proposition definition; Narrative development; Financial results framing; Earnings release drafting |
| <b>Earnings Management</b> | Earnings releases, conference call scripts | Quarterly earnings support; Conference call logistics; Script development; Analyst commentary management |
| <b>Analyst & Investor Relations</b> | Analyst outreach, investor targeting | Analyst coverage cultivation; Investor identification and targeting; Relationship management; Analyst briefings |
| <b>Investor Events</b> | Roadshows, investor days, conferences | Event strategy and execution; Investor meetings; Non-deal roadshows; Conference presentations |
| <b>Research & Insights</b> | Investor perception audits, market intel | Real-time monitoring; Peer financial reporting; Market insights; Investor sentiment analysis |
| <b>Governance & Support</b> | Calendar planning, event management | IR calendar management; Website IR section; Investor communication planning |
| <b>Crisis & Special Situations</b> | Crisis management, IPO support | Crisis communications; IPO/SPO quiet period management; Succession planning; M&A communications |
| <b>ESG & Positioning</b> | ESG strategy, company positioning | ESG messaging; Sustainability communications; Market positioning |
