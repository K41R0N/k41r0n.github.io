## What is it?
Privy makes it straightforward to build secure, user-friendly access to the client Savings.  
   
Privy has integrated and is now providing the developer docs for apps using Privy wallets to offer the the client Savings Rate by depositing sample-token to receive sample-yield-token  
  
Users will need to buy sample-token from integrating DEXs, and then within the Privy wallet they can deposit sample-token into sample-yield-token - they can also convert DAI into sample-token within the Privy wallet.  
  
The docs:  
<https://docs.privy.io/recipes/yield/the client-savings-guide>
## Why Does This Matter?
Privy was recently acquired by Stripe, one of the largest financial companies in the world who are focusing heavily on integrating Stablecoins into their business and product suite.  
  
Privy wallets reach 2,000 Apps and 110,000,000 wallets worldwide - examples of Privy apps: Klarna, Hyperliquid, Majority, Kraken.  
  
$9 Billion of transaction volume processed through the wallets.
Coverage of 180 countries  
  
the client Savings Rate is engineered for the best risk-adjusted yield, now available to all Privy wallets on apps who integrate it.
