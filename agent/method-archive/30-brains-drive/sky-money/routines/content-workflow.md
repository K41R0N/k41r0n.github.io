# Content Workflow — the client product

---

## How Content Gets Made

1. **Brief** — [[../people/the author-arango|the author]] creates the brief (mandatory since April 9, 2026 — No Brief = No Work, decision: [[../memory/decisions/decisions-log#2026-04-09 — No Brief = No Work Policy]])
2. **Draft** — [[../people/daniel-demian|Daniel Demian]] creates first draft following the [[protocols/copywriting-guidelines/README|5-pass copywriting method]] and [[../memory/strategy/concepts/brand-voice|brand voice]]
3. **Design** — [[../people/krystee|Krystee]] creates visual assets (Director of Design, the client product)
4. **PR** — [[../people/kyle-heise|a stakeholder Heise]] handles earned media / press if applicable
5. **Review** — [[../people/the author-arango|the author]] reviews for strategy alignment, brand voice, legal compliance
6. **Legal check** — If claims about yield/safety, flag for a legal reviewer review via [[../people/jacek-czarnecki|Jacek]] (see [[../memory/strategy/concepts/legal-guidelines]])
7. **Approve** — [[../people/the author-arango|the author]] signs off (sole approver)
8. **Publish** — [[../people/daniel-demian|Daniel]] schedules and publishes
9. **Log** — Published content gets logged in assets/social-calendar

## Quality Checklist

Before any content goes live:
- [ ] Brief exists (No Brief = No Work — [[../people/raul-chisluca|Raul]] mandate, April 9, 2026)
- [ ] Follows Headline → Benefit → Proof structure (see [[../memory/strategy/concepts/brand-voice#Copywriting Pattern]])
- [ ] Uses only "Words That Work" (see [[../memory/strategy/concepts/brand-voice#Words That Work]])
- [ ] No prohibited terms from [[../memory/strategy/concepts/legal-guidelines|legal guidelines]]
- [ ] All claims verifiable onchain or from confirmed sources
- [ ] No future-state promises (Containers, Halos, NFATs, Synome)
- [ ] No mention of agents as partners (a service agent, a service agent, a service agent — they're [[../memory/strategy/concepts/competitive-landscape|competitors]])
- [ ] Follows [[../memory/strategy/concepts/storytelling-and-ai-discipline|storytelling standard]]
- [ ] [[../people/the author-arango|the author]] sign-off confirmed
