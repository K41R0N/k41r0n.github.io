# Pass 1: Big Idea (Ogilvy)

**Purpose:** Identify the single compelling idea that anchors all copy. Based on David Ogilvy's principle that every ad should be part of a long-term campaign built on one big idea.

**When to use:** At the start of every copywriting task, before writing a single word of the draft.

---

## The Ogilvy Principle

David Ogilvy said: "Every ad should be part of a long-term campaign built on one big idea." The big idea is:

- **Simple** — expressible in one sentence
- **Compelling** — makes the reader want to know more
- **Unique** — differentiates this brand from competitors
- **Enduring** — can sustain multiple pieces of content over time

---

## How to Find the Big Idea

### Step 1: Read the Brief

The brief (created by the author, per "No Brief = No Work" rule) should state:
- The topic
- The angle
- The format
- The audience
- The goal

If the brief doesn't exist, stop and ask the author. Do not proceed without a brief.

### Step 2: Identify the Core Message

Ask: "What is the ONE thing I want the reader to remember after finishing this copy?"

Examples:
- **sample-yield-token launch:** "sample-yield-token is the safest way to earn yield on stablecoins."
- **the client Eco positioning:** "the client is where capital never sits still."
- **sample-rate explanation:** "The the client Savings Rate is why the client exists — it's the core differentiator from a competitor."

### Step 3: Test the Big Idea

A valid big idea passes these tests:
- **One sentence:** Can you state it in 15 words or fewer?
- **Differentiation:** Does it distinguish this brand from competitors?
- **Audience relevance:** Does it matter to the target audience?
- **Brand alignment:** Does it align with `positioning.md` and `brand-voice.md`?

If the answer to any test is "no," refine the big idea before proceeding.

---

## Output of Pass 1

A one-sentence statement of the big idea. Write it at the top of your draft document. Everything in the copy serves this idea.

**Example:**

```
BIG IDEA: sample-yield-token is the safest way to earn yield on stablecoins — curated, transparent, and backed by the the client Savings Rate.
```

---

## Common Mistakes

- **Multiple big ideas:** If you have two big ideas, you have zero. Pick one.
- **Vague big idea:** "the client is great" is not a big idea. "sample-yield-token offers the best risk-adjusted yield in DeFi" is a big idea.
- **Big idea doesn't match brief:** If the brief is about IR newsletters, the big idea should be about investor communication, not product features.
