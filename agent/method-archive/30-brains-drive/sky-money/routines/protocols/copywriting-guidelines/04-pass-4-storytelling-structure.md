# Pass 4: Storytelling Structure

**Purpose:** Structure the copy as a narrative, not a bullet list. Explain why numbers moved, not just what they are. Create narrative connection between claims.

**When to use:** After Pass 3 (Voice Matching) is complete. Restructure the draft for narrative flow.

---

## The Rule

Storytelling means explaining **why**, not just **what**. If you list metrics without explaining their significance, you're writing a report, not telling a story.

Per a stakeholder's policy: "I would rather give them a bunch of questions than a bunch of AI written generic thoughts."

---

## What AI-Generated Filler Looks Like

### Structural Tells

- **Equal-length paragraphs:** Every paragraph is 3-4 sentences, same shape, no variation
- **Bullet lists:** Every bullet is the same structure, no narrative connection between them
- **Metric dumping:** List of numbers with no explanation of why they matter or how they changed

### Phrase Tells

- "demonstrating strong growth"
- "underscoring the importance of X"
- "continued momentum in Y"
- "strong fundamentals"
- "leveraging our position in Z"
- "harnessing the power of W"
- "key metrics show improvement"

These phrases say nothing. Replace them with specifics.

---

## How to Build Narrative

### Step 1: Identify the Story Arc

Every piece of copy should have a narrative arc:
- **Hook:** Why should the reader care? (Connected to the Big Idea from Pass 1)
- **Context:** What's the situation? (Factual anchoring from Pass 2)
- **Conflict/Challenge:** What problem does this solve? (Positioning from Step 0a)
- **Resolution:** How does this brand/product address it? (Product facts from entity files)
- **Call to action:** What should the reader do next?

### Step 2: Connect Claims with Narrative

Instead of:
> "sample-yield-token TVL is $500M. The yield is 5%. The risk is low."

Write:
> "sample-yield-token has reached $500M TVL, driven by its 5% yield — the best risk-adjusted return in stablecoin DeFi. The low risk profile comes from the the client Savings Rate backing, which differentiates sample-yield-token from algorithmic stablecoins."

The second version explains **why** the numbers matter and **how** they connect.

### Step 3: Vary Sentence Structure

Mix short and long sentences. Use short sentences for impact. Use long sentences for explanation. Avoid uniform paragraph lengths.

### Step 4: Explain Number Movements

If a number changed, explain why:
- Did governance vote to change the sample-rate rate?
- Did a new vault launch increase TVL?
- Did market conditions shift yield?

If you don't know why a number moved, **leave a comment and tag the person who knows**. Do not write generic filler.

---

## Common Storytelling Mistakes

- **Bullet list masquerading as prose:** Converting bullets to sentences without adding narrative connection
- **Report-style writing:** Listing facts without explaining their significance
- **No hook:** Starting with product features instead of why the reader should care
- **No resolution:** Ending without a clear takeaway or call to action
- **Uniform structure:** Every paragraph is the same length, every sentence is the same complexity

---

## Output of Pass 4

A draft with narrative flow — each sentence leads to the next, building toward the big idea. Numbers are explained, not just listed. The reader understands **why** this matters.
