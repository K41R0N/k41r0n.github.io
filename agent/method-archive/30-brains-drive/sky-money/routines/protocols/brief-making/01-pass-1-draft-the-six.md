# Pass 1 — Draft the Six

**Purpose:** Answer all six questions in order. Each answer is short, specific, and earns its place.

**Last updated:** 2026-06-13
**Approver:** the author

---

## The Rule

**Every line in a brief earns its place by changing what someone does.**

---

## Drafting Each Question

1. **What is it?** 1-3 sentences. Lead with what's actually happening, in plain language.
2. **Why is it happening?** 1-3 sentences. The business reason, problem being solved, or opportunity.
3. **Who is involved?** Names/roles only. Bullets acceptable. Cap at 5-6 names.
4. **When is it happening?** Specific dates. Internal deadline and external deadline.
5. **Where is it happening?** Literal or digital surfaces. e.g., "X, LinkedIn, the client product marketing site".
6. **How do we plan on doing it?** 2-4 sentences describing the execution strategy, framing, sequence, and key constraint.
