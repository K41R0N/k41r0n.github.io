# Pass 2 — Numbers Verification

**Purpose:** Every figure in the brief is traced to a live source or removed.

**Last updated:** 2026-06-13
**Approver:** the author

---

## The Rule

**Never invent a number. Never use a stale number. Verify at the moment of writing.**

---

## How to Verify

1. **Identify the live source.** For the client financial data: https://client-example.invalid or https://client-example.invalid.
2. **Fetch the current value.** Not last week's. The value at moment of writing.
3. **Annotate the brief working copy** with the source URL.
4. **If the source doesn't show it:** escalate. Do not estimate. Do not say "approximately."
