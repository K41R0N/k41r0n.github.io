# Content Workflow — the client

---

## How Content Gets Made

1. **Brief** — [[../people/the author-arango|the author]] or [[../people/kyle-reidhead|a stakeholder R]] defines the content need
2. **Draft** — Assigned team member creates first draft following the 5-pass copywriting method (see [[protocols/copywriting-guidelines/README|Readme]])
3. **Review** — the author reviews for strategy alignment and legal compliance
4. **Agent coordination** — If content references specific agents, coordinate with agent teams
5. **Legal check** — If claims about protocol performance, flag for review
6. **Approve** — the author signs off
7. **Publish** — Execute through appropriate channel
8. **Log** — Published content gets logged

---

## Quality Checklist

Before any ecosystem content goes live:
- [ ] Institutional tone (not casual crypto Twitter)
- [ ] Agent independence respected (not speaking FOR agents)
- [ ] No future-state promises (Containers, Halos, NFATs)
- [ ] No "Central Bank" narrative confirmation
- [ ] No Maker legacy invocation
- [ ] All claims verifiable
- [ ] Legal guidelines respected
