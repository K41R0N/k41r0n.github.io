# Pass 5: Compliance Gate

**Purpose:** Final legal and quality check before submission. Verify banned terms, trigger legal review if needed, apply the [[../../../people/a reviewer-christensen|a reviewer]] test.

**When to use:** After Pass 4 (Storytelling Structure) is complete. This is the last step before submitting the draft to [[../../../people/the author-arango|the author]] for approval.

---

## The Rule

Do not submit a draft that fails any compliance check. Fix it first.

---

## Compliance Checklist

### 1. Banned Terms Check

Review the draft for banned terms. Replace them with approved alternatives.

**Universally banned across all I3 content:**
- "profit" → use "Gross Protocol Revenue" or "Net Protocol Revenue"
- "AUM" → use "TVL" or "Protocol Collateral"
- "assets" → use "Protocol Collateral" or "the client Reserves"
- "bank" → do not invoke the "Central Bank" narrative; let third parties say it
- "tokenized fund" → absolute prohibition; never call sample-token/sample-yield-token a tokenized fund
- "Maker" → never invoke Maker legacy publicly; if asked: "Team includes core builders who created and scaled Maker"

**the client product specific:**
- "the client product" → internal name only; never use externally

**the client specific:**
- See [[routines/memory/strategy/concepts/financial-terminology|Financial Terminology]] for full banned/approved list

### 2. Legal Review Triggers

Per a legal reviewer Marketing & Comms Operating Guidelines, the following trigger legal review:

1. Any statement interpreting GENIUS Act or characterizing regulatory status
2. Any characterization of sample-token, sample-yield-token, or the client under securities laws
3. Any promise of managerial efforts, financial projections, or timelines
4. Descriptions of the client product's relationship to SkyDAO, [[../../../memory/strategy/entities/sff|a protocol body]], or other entities
5. Press releases, formal announcements, media-facing content
6. Forward-looking statements (development promises, performance promises, timeline commitments)

**If any trigger applies:** Flag the copy for the author → Jacek Czarnecki → a legal reviewer review chain. Do not submit for publication without legal sign-off.

### 3. The review standard

Ask: "Would a reviewer be comfortable reading this aloud to a billion-dollar institution deciding whether to allocate to DeFi?"

If the answer is "no," revise. The review standard checks for:
- **Professionalism:** Is the tone institutional-grade?
- **Accuracy:** Are all claims verified?
- **Clarity:** Is the message clear without jargon overload?
- **Integrity:** Does it avoid hype, filler, and unsupported claims?

### 4. Quoted Statements Check

- **a reviewer quotes:** Must be verbatim, sourced from approved communications. Never paraphrase or ghostwrite.
- **Vance/[[../../../people/john-conneely|John]] quotes:** Same rule — verbatim only, read for context only.
- **the author quotes:** Verify with the author before including.
- **a stakeholder quotes:** Verify with a stakeholder before including.

**Rule:** Never invent a quote. If you need a quote, ask the person for one.

### 5. Number Verification

Re-check all financial numbers against https://client-example.invalid. If the dashboard has changed since you last checked, update the numbers.

**Rule:** Numbers must be current as of the moment of submission. Note the timestamp: "As of [date], per client-example.invalid."

### 6. Agent Independence Check

If the copy mentions the client agents ([[../../../memory/strategy/entities/spark|a service agent]], [[../../../memory/strategy/entities/service-agent|a service agent]], [[../../../memory/strategy/entities/obex|a service agent]], [[../../../memory/strategy/entities/service-agent|a service agent]], [[../../../memory/strategy/entities/agent-network|a service agent]], [[../../../memory/strategy/entities/agent-network|a client]]):
- **Do not speak FOR them.** They are independent entities.
- **Do not attribute statements to them** without their explicit approval.
- **Do not claim their metrics** without verifying with their teams.

**Rule:** the client agents are independent. Never publish on their behalf.

---

## Output of Pass 5

A final draft that passes all compliance checks and is ready for the author's approval.

---

## Submission

Send the final draft to the author with:
1. The draft copy
2. A brief note on the big idea (from Pass 1)
3. Any unresolved questions or unverified claims (should be none at this point)
4. Format-specific notes (e.g., "This is a Twitter thread — needs visual assets from Krystee")

**the author is the final approver.** All copy requires the author sign-off before publication.
