# Pass 4 — Cut to One Page

**Purpose:** Enforce the one-page constraint by pruning.

**Last updated:** 2026-06-13
**Approver:** [[../../../people/the author-arango|the author]] Arango

---

## The Rule

**One page maximum. Non-negotiable.**

---

## The Cut Test

For every sentence in the brief, ask:
> *If I removed this, would someone do something different?*
If no, cut.

Apply ruthlessly to each question. Remove metadata, political flags, escalation language, commercial context, and operational micro-coordination.
