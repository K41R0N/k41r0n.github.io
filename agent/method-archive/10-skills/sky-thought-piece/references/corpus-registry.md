# Corpus Registry: Spent Lines and Owned Anchors

Added 2026-08-25. A new tell class emerged once the corpus grew past one set: **cross-artifact collisions**. The founding registry checked redundancy within a set; by the fourth artifact, the same climax shapes were re-landing across different bylines (a negation-reframe climax and a "pull the answers yourself" dashboard close each reached third use across three bylines before a reviewer caught them). A model re-lands its best lines; a human editor notices they already spent them. Sweep every new draft against this file, and append to it at every delivery.

## Seed-echo (the intra-piece cousin of collision)

New material written around seed paragraphs tends to rhyme with them verbatim: Capital Routes V3's new sections duplicated "published before it took effect," "because the terms were public before...," and "behind the same delay" straight out of the seed. Echoing a seed motif is cohesion; duplicating its frame is a tell. Keep the payoff, vary the frame ("on terms neither of them wrote and both could read before they arrived" pays off "the terms were public before anything entered the line" without reprinting it). Verification reviewers should hunt seed-echoes explicitly.
