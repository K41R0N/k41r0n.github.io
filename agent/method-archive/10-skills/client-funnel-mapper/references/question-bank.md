# Pre-call question bank

Pick what's still unanswered, rewrite each question with the specific facts you found (name the emails, numbers and URLs), and group by area so different people on the client side can take a section. Aim for 20 to 30 questions. If that's too many, suggest sending the sections that decide the scope in advance and keeping the rest for the call.

Open with two intro options, a warm one and a concise one, that link the funnel map and ask the client to bring answers, data or access.

## 1. The funnel map
- Is anything on the map missing or wrong: channels, steps, tools or owners?

## 2. Email today
- Which tool sends each email? Can we get access, even view-only, before we start?
- A full list of every email going out, automated and manual, with audience, trigger or schedule, and sender. (List what you've already found and ask what else exists.)
- List sizes by segment, and how many subscribers are engaged (opened in the last 90 days).
- The last three months of performance per email: opens, clicks, unsubscribes, bounces, spam complaints. Any deliverability issues?
- Who writes, builds and approves each email? Who is our day-to-day contact?
- Is there an existing strategy, calendar, template set or tone guide?

## 3. New subscribers or members
- Does onboarding change based on whether people complete the activation steps? What happens after the last email in the sequence?
- What share of new signups activate, and does activation trigger anything?
- Is each list reaching the audience it's meant for? (Name any mismatch from the audit.)
- Given the goal for this period, are you open to pausing or changing [specific email]?

## 4. The campaign
- Which campaign emails are written or scheduled, and which are open for us to shape?
- How do campaign emails sit alongside the regular newsletters? Is there a maximum number of sends per week?
- Rules, eligibility, late entry and legal sign-off status. Can we start teasing now?
- Can the email tool segment by activation, campaign participation, tier and signup source?

## 5. The conversion moment
- Is the offer confirmed: products, prices, dates, which lists?
- How did past conversion campaigns perform?

## 6. Tracking and data
- How are signups tracked by source today, and from what data source? Can we get dashboard and analytics access?
- Status and timeline of known tracking fixes. (Name them.)
- Does this split work: we own and report the channel and email metrics, you share signup numbers weekly?

## 7. Other channels
- For each channel with a gap on the map (e.g. YouTube with no signup link): who manages it, can the fix go in this week, and what data exists?
- Lead magnets and tools: which ones convert, and what's blocking them?
- Community (Discord, Slack, forum): who has access, who runs it?
- Affiliates and creators: programme status, terms, referral tracking, and whether we can invite new partners.
- Paid media: anything running or planned, and budget?

## 8. Ways of working
- Approval flow and turnaround, and compliance guardrails to build in from day one.
- Who implements our recommendations on pages and channels, and how quickly?
