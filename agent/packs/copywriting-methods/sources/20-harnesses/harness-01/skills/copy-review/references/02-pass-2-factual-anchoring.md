# Pass 2: Factual Anchoring

**Purpose:** Make factual claims traceable to evidence. This pass checks support; it does not decide whether a supported claim is allowed.

**When to use:** After the big idea, across every factual claim in the draft.

## The rule

Every factual claim needs a source a reviewer can inspect. If it cannot be verified, mark it `[UNVERIFIED]` and identify the source or person needed. Resolve unverified claims before voice review.

Claims include numbers, capabilities, personnel and roles, timelines, comparisons, superlatives and statements about law or regulation.

## How to anchor claims

1. **Identify claims.** Read each sentence and ask, "Can I prove this?"
2. **Trace evidence.** Use the project's current source hierarchy and prefer direct, current sources. Note the exact source, section and date where helpful.
3. **Cite clearly.** Put a source note beside each claim or in a linked review record. Ensure the reviewer can reach it.
4. **Flag gaps.** Use `[UNVERIFIED: source or reviewer needed]`. Do not proceed to voice review while a material claim remains unresolved.

A source citation does not override approved claim restrictions, privacy limits, or the source hierarchy.

## What not to do

- Do not invent or estimate a number.
- Recheck time-sensitive figures at the point of use.
- Do not paraphrase a quote as though it were exact.
- Do not attribute a statement to someone who did not make it.
- Do not claim a capability that current product evidence does not support.
- Do not use a source merely because it supports a preferred claim; check whether it is current and authoritative for that claim.

## Output

A draft with traceable sources for each factual claim and explicit flags for unresolved items.
