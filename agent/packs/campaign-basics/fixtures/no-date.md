# Campaign plan
asker: beginner
## The one thing
The weekend bread class.
## Who it is for
Neighbours who want to bake but have never tried.
## The one idea
Anyone can make good bread with five minutes of work.
## The one action
Book a seat for the first class.
## What we are not
- not: a bakery. We teach, we do not sell loaves.
## Where
- place: neighbourhood group chat | job: get the first ten bookings
- place: flyer at the cafe | job: reach people who are not online
## How we will know
- measure: seats booked | target: 10
